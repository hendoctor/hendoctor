## Hey, I'm James.

I lead engineering and enterprise operations at **Centralized Supply Chain Services**, where we build technology behind a multi-billion-dollar restaurant supply-chain ecosystem.

I also founded **[RootedAI](https://rootedai.tech)** because I think most companies are dramatically underestimating what small teams can build with AI.

I write code. I lead engineers. I design systems. I automate things that annoy me.

Sometimes those become companies.

## What I'm building

🤖 **AI agents** that actually do work  
🏗️ **Enterprise systems** that replace spreadsheets, email chains, and tribal knowledge  
🌱 **RootedAI** products and client platforms  
🪺 **Nestwise** product intelligence for families  
⚙️ **Developer tooling** for AI-native engineering  
🧪 **Side projects** that usually start with *"wait, couldn't I just build this?"*

My favorite architecture:

```text
messy problem → software → automation → AI → delete unnecessary work
```

## Things I believe

**AI isn't autocomplete.** It's becoming part of the engineering organization.

**Complexity has to earn its keep.**

**Automate boring work before hiring humans to do more boring work.**

**Engineering leaders should still understand the code.**

**The best internal tool is usually the one nobody needs training to use.**

**Shipping teaches you things architecture diagrams can't.**

And:

> If I have to do something annoying three times, there's a decent chance I'm going to build software for it.
